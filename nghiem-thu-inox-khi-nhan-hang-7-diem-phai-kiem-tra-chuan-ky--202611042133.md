# Technical Summary: Stainless Steel Receiving Inspection Protocol

A comprehensive technical specification and receiving protocol for verifying stainless steel materials at job sites, ensuring strict compliance with ASTM standards and contractual obligations.

## Core Technical Parameters & Specifications
- **Standard Reference:** ASTM A480 tolerance guidelines for cold-rolled stainless steel sheets.
- **Measurement Tool:** Digital Micrometer with Ratchet Stop (±0.001mm resolution) to eliminate mechanical clamping errors.
- **Material Verification:** XRF Spectrometer testing for alloy composition (e.g., verifying Molybdenum content ≥ 2.0% for Grade 316).
- **Documentation Traceability:** Mill Test Certificate (EN 10204 Type 3.1) matched with physical Heat Numbers.

## 7-Point Inspection Checklist
1. General packaging and protective film integrity check.
2. Precision thickness measurement via digital micrometer.
3. Dimensional and geometric tolerance control.
4. Surface flatness assessment for Fiber Laser cutting.
5. Spectroscopic alloy verification (XRF / Chemical test).
6. Random visual inspection (10% - 20% sample opening).
7. Cross-referencing Packing List, Heat Number, and Mill Certificate 3.1.
