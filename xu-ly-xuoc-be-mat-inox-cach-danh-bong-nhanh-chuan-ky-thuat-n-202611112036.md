# Technical Summary: Stainless Steel Surface Defect Restoration & Polishing

## Overview
This documentation outlines standard engineering procedures for restoring scratched stainless steel surfaces, covering mechanical defect classification, grit progression sequences, and optical finishing parameters for industrial and architectural applications.

## Core Specifications & Parameters
- **Surface Finish Types:** Hairline (No.4), BA/No.8 Mirror, and 2B Industrial Matte.
- **Grit Progression Range:** P180 to P3000 (Silicon Carbide / Aluminum Oxide).
- **Optical Roughness Target for Mirrors:** Ra <= 0.02 micromet (μm).
- **Defect Classification:** 
  - Level 1 (< 5 μm): Scotch-Brite non-woven pads and finishing compounds.
  - Level 2 (5 - 25 μm): P400 to P800 waterproof sandpaper.
  - Level 3 (25 - 50 μm): Coarse grinding from P180 to P240.

## Industrial Applications
- Elevator cabin manufacturing and architectural wall panels.
- Commercial kitchen equipment and stainless steel casework.
- Storage tanks and sanitary piping systems for food processing.
