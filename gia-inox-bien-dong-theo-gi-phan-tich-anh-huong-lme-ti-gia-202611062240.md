# Technical Summary: Stainless Steel Price Fluctuations & Macro Drivers

This technical brief outlines the core metallurgical and macroeconomic factors driving stainless steel pricing mechanisms, designed for mechanical engineers and industrial procurement teams.

### Core Parameters & Cost Drivers
- **LME Nickel Index:** Primary determinant of austenitic stainless steel grades (e.g., Inox 304/316), directly influencing the monthly Alloy Surcharge formulation.
- **Foreign Exchange (USD/VND):** Impacts direct import settlement costs for raw coils and sheets sourced from global mills (Posco, Nippon Steel, Tisco).
- **Logistics & Ocean Freight:** Heavy-density cargo dynamics affecting landed costs through container shipping rates and heavy-lift charges.
- **Trade Defense Policies:** Anti-dumping duties and regional supply-demand balances dictating market differentiation.

### Material Applications
- Suitable for industrial tank fabrication, food-grade processing equipment, and M&E mechanical construction.
- Optimal material selection based on operating environments to mitigate project cost overruns.
