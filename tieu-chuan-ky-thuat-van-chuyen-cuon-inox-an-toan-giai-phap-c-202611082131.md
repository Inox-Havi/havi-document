# Technical Summary: Steel Coil Logistics & Surface Protection Protocol

## Core Specifications & Overview
This document outlines engineering standards for safe transportation and handling of industrial stainless steel coils (SUS 304, SUS 316) weighing 3 to 12 tons. Compliance with ASTM A480 / EN 10088 standards ensures zero mechanical deformation (edge pinching) and micro-fretting surface damage during transit.

## Technical Parameters & Logistics Guidelines

- **Weight & Contact Profile:** Jumbo coils (1,000mm - 1,500mm width) contact flatbed trailers via narrow line contact, requiring dynamic force mitigation.
- **Saddle Fixture:** Mandatory use of Coil Saddles (V-blocks with 35° - 45° bevel angle) paired with high friction rubber mats ($μ ≥ 0.6$).
- **Edge Protection:** L-shaped galvanized steel/engineering plastic edge protectors to distribute high-tensile steel strapping pressure (19mm/32mm width).
- **Moisture Control:** 3-layer anti-corrosion barrier: PE/Laser protective film, VCI anti-moisture paper with silica gel, and waterproof 2-layer canvas.
- **Lifting Equipment:** C-hook with 20mm Polyurethane cushion or Coil Ram attachments; fork tines directly inside eye cores are strictly prohibited.

## Applications
- Precision stamping & slitting lines
- HVAC duct manufacturing
- Commercial kitchen equipment fabrication
- Architectural & elevator cabin cladding
