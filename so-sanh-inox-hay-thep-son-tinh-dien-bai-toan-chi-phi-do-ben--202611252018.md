# Technical Summary: Inox vs. Powder-Coated Carbon Steel

A comparative technical evaluation of corrosion mechanisms, lifecycle costs (LCC), and material performance for industrial and architectural engineering applications.

## Core Specifications & Performance
- **Corrosion Resistance**: 
  - *Inox (SUS 304)*: Permanent self-healing passive film ($Cr_2O_3$) containing min 18% Cr and 8% Ni; impervious to UV and chemical degradation.
  - *Powder-Coated Steel*: Organic polymer barrier (60-120 µm) over carbon steel; susceptible to Filiform Corrosion upon mechanical scratching.
- **Lifecycle Breakeven Point**: Years 5-6, after which cumulative maintenance costs of painted steel exceed initial CapEx of stainless steel.
- **Thermal & Mechanical Limits**: Inox maintains structural integrity from -196°C to 870°C (Fire rating A1); organic coatings soften above 120°C.

## Recommended Applications
- **Exterior / Harsh Environments**: SUS 304 / SUS 316 for industrial tanks, cable trays, and structural cladding.
- **Indoor / Budget-Optimized**: SUS 201 for dry storage racks and internal enclosures.
- **Processed by**: HaVi Việt Nam CNC Laser Cutting, CNC Bending, and TIG/MIG Welding services.
