# Technical Summary: Sheet Metal Fabrication of Stainless Steel

## Overview
This document outlines the precision engineering workflow for stainless steel sheet metal fabrication based on CAD drawings. The process integrates automated nesting, high-power Fiber laser cutting, multi-axis hydraulic bending, and high-integrity welding to achieve strict dimensional tolerances ranging from ±0.05mm to ±0.1mm.

## Technical Specifications & Parameters
- **Material Grades:** SUS 304, SUS 316, SUS 201, SUS 430 (Certified CO/CQ).
- **Standard Sheet Sizes:** 1219x2438 mm, 1500x3000 mm, 1500x6000 mm.
- **Cutting Technology:** High-power Fiber Laser with High-pressure Nitrogen (N2) assist.
- **Bending Technology:** Multi-axis hydraulic CNC press brakes.
- **Quality Standards:** ISO 2768-m (Linear & geometric tolerances), ISO 9013 (Thermal cut quality).

## Key Fabrication Stages
- **CAD/CAM Processing:** Automated part nesting for optimal material utilization.
- **Thermal Cutting:** Elimination of oxide scale via inert gas shielding.
- **Forming & Finishing:** Controlled heat and force application to prevent thermal distortion and surface defects.

## Industrial Applications
- Electrical enclosures and control panels.
- Industrial tanks, pressure vessels, and sanitary piping systems.
- Custom mechanical assemblies and architectural framing.
