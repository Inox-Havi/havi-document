# Technical Summary: PE Protective Film & Adhesive Removal for Stainless Steel Sheets

## Core Specifications
- **Material Types**: Co-extruded Multi-layer PE Film & Laser Fiber Film (Thickness: 50 µm - 100 µm).
- **Adhesive System**: High-grade acrylic pressure-sensitive adhesive (PSA).
- **Optimal Removal Window**: Immediately after final installation and before exceeding 3-6 months exposure to prevent UV cross-linking.
- **Compatible Finishes**: No.4, Hairline, BA, Mirror 8K.

## Key Technical Applications & Solutions
- **Mechanical Protection**: Acts as an elastic damping layer against fretting scratches during handling and transport.
- **Fabrication Support**: Absorbs contact stress during hydraulic press bending and prevents mechanical indentations.
- **Laser Cutting**: Absorbs 1.064 nm laser wavelengths cleanly without bubble formation or burr creation using specialized Laser Film.
- **Adhesive Removal Protocols**: Thermal gun softening (60°C - 80°C), Isopropyl Alcohol (IPA) 90%+, d-Limonene citrus-based solvents, and 3M industrial cleaners.
