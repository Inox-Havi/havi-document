# Technical Summary: ASTM A480 Stainless Steel Plate Tolerances

- Standard Specification: ASTM A480 / A480M for flat-rolled stainless and heat-resisting steel plate, sheet, and strip.
- Key Parameters Controlled: Thickness tolerance, width, length, edge camber, and flatness.
- Cold-Rolled (Sheet) Tolerances: Typically ranges from ±0.02mm to ±0.15mm depending on dimensions; measured strictly away from edge drop zones.
- Hot-Rolled (Plate) Tolerances: Applies positive bias rules with strict limitations on negative deviations to maintain structural integrity and pressure vessel safety.
