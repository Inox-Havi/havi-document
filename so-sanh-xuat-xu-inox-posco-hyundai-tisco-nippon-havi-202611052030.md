# Technical Summary: Stainless Steel Origin Evaluation (Posco, Hyundai, Nippon, Tisco)

This technical document outlines the metallurgical properties, thickness tolerances, and mechanical behaviors of cold-rolled austenitic stainless steel (SUS 304/304L) supplied by major global mills.

### Core Engineering Parameters:
- **Posco & Nippon Steel**: Exceptional purity via VOD/AOD vacuum refining; ultra-low negative thickness tolerances (-0.02mm to -0.04mm per ASTM A480); superior elongation for deep drawing.
- **Hyundai BNG Steel**: Advanced cold-rolling technology providing superior surface finish profiles (2B, BA, Hairline) optimized for architectural and high-aesthetic applications.
- **Tisco**: Cost-effective material stream suited for general structural fabrication and standard industrial frameworks.

### Verification Protocols:
- Mill Test Certificate compliance per EN 10204 3.1.
- Heat Number traceability across coil edges and documentation.
- Spectroscopic PMI (XRF) testing confirming minimum Cr ≥ 18.0% and Ni ≥ 8.0%.
