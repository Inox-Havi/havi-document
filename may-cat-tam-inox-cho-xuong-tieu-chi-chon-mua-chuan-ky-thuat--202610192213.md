# Technical Summary: Stainless Steel Sheet Cutting Machine Criteria

## Core Specifications & Parameters
- **Material Scope:** Stainless Steel (AISI 304, 316, 201), tensile strength 520 - 750 MPa.
- **Laser Power Ratio:** Requires 1.4x - 1.6x higher power output compared to carbon steel of identical thickness to prevent thermal reflection issues and edge discoloration.
- **Assist Gas Standard:** High-pressure Nitrogen ($N_2$) at 16 - 22 bar for oxidation-free, bright, clean edges.
- **Mechanical Shear Parameters:** Blade clearance set at 8% - 10% of sheet thickness, utilizing alloy steels (Cr12MoV, D2, H13) to prevent chipping.

## Key Technical Matrix
- **Laser Fiber CNC:** High precision, ideal for complex contours, automated production.
- **Hydraulic Shear:** Cost-effective for linear cutting of thin-to-medium gauges (150 - 300 million VND investment range).
- **Plasma HD:** Heavy structural fabrication with higher thermal input.
