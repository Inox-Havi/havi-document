# Technical Summary: Stainless Steel Pickling & Passivation (ASTM A380 / A967)

## Overview
Chemical surface treatment protocol for stainless steel welds and industrial plates to eliminate heat tint, remove free iron, and restore the passive chromium oxide (Cr2O3) protective layer.

## Key Parameters & Specifications
- **Primary Standards:** ASTM A380, ASTM A967.
- **Pickling Agent:** Hydrofluoric acid (HF) + Nitric acid (HNO3); removes 1-3 micromets of surface metal.
- **Passivation Agent:** 15%-25% Nitric acid or 4%-10% Citric acid; targets free iron without base metal etching.
- **Water Rinse Quality:** Chloride ion (Cl-) concentration strictly below 50 ppm.
- **Application Fields:** Food processing tanks, pharmaceutical pipelines, chemical and marine engineering.
