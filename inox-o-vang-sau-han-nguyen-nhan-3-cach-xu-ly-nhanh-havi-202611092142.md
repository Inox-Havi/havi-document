# Technical Summary: Inox Weld Heat Tint Analysis & Treatment

## Overview
This technical brief details the metallurgical mechanisms behind weld discoloration (Heat Tint) in austenitic stainless steels (SUS 304 / SUS 316) within the Heat Affected Zone (HAZ) at temperatures ranging from 300°C to 800°C.

## Key Parameters & Specifications
- **Critical Oxidation Range:** 300°C – 800°C
- **Sensitization Range:** 450°C – 850°C (Chromium Carbide precipitation Cr23C6)
- **Shielding Gas Requirement:** Pure Argon >= 99.99%, Flow rate 8–18 L/min with proper Back Purging (<50 ppm O2).

## Treatment Methodologies
- **Electrochemical Cleaning:** AC/DC current with phosphoric acid and carbon fiber brush.
- **Chemical Gel Pickling:** Acid mixtures (HNO3 & HF) for oxide removal.
- **Mechanical Polishing & Passivation:** Restoring the Cr2O3 passive layer.
