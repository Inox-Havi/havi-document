# Technical Summary: Stainless Steel Plate 304 - Thickness 5.0mm

## Overview
The 5.0mm thickness 304 stainless steel hot-rolled plate (No.1 finish) is engineered for heavy-duty industrial structural applications. It complies strictly with ASTM A240/A480 standards, offering superior corrosion resistance and mechanical performance.

## Core Specifications
- **Material Grade:** Austenitic Stainless Steel 304
- **Thickness:** 5.0 mm
- **Surface Finish:** No.1 (Hot Rolled, Annealed & Pickled)
- **Nominal Weight:** 39.65 kg/m²
- **Yield Strength:** ≥ 205 MPa
- **Tensile Strength:** ≥ 515 MPa
- **Elongation:** ≥ 40%

## Primary Industrial Applications
- Structural base plates and column footings in corrosive environments.
- Pressure vessels and tanks (5m³ to 25m³) for chemical, food, and beverage plants.
- Laser-cut slip-on and blind flanges (ANSI standards).
- Wear-resistant liners for material handling chutes in cement and thermal power plants.
