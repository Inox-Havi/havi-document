# Technical Summary: Food Grade Stainless Steel Surface Cleaning & CIP Protocol

## Overview
A comprehensive technical guide for F&B industrial equipment maintenance, focusing on chemical cleaning protocols and surface roughness standards to prevent Biofilm formation and preserve passive chromium oxide layers.

## Key Technical Specifications
- **Surface Roughness Standard**: Ra ≤ 0.8 µm (Standard), Ra ≤ 0.4 µm (Pharmaceutical/Aseptic).
- **CIP Sequence**: Pre-rinse (50-60°C) -> Caustic Wash (NaOH 1.5-2.0%, 75-85°C) -> Intermediate Rinse -> Acid Passivation (HNO₃/H₃PO₄, 60°C) -> Sanitization (PAA 150-200 ppm).
- **Material Grade**: SUS 304 and SUS 316L (Food Grade compliant with FDA & EHEDG).
- **Sanitizer Preferred**: Peracetic Acid (CH₃COOOH - PAA) as a no-rinse alternative to prevent Pitting Corrosion caused by free chlorine.

## Applications
- Dairy processing plants, brewery tanks, beverage pipelines, and food manufacturing automated conveyor systems provided by HaVi Việt Nam.
