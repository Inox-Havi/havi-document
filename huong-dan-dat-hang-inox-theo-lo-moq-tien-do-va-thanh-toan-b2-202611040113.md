# Technical Summary: Bulk Stainless Steel Order Specification

A comprehensive supply framework for batch-ordered stainless steel materials, ensuring metallurgical consistency, strict dimensional tolerances, and optimal cost-efficiency for industrial engineering projects.

## Core Technical Specifications & Parameters
- **Material Grades:** Austenitic & Ferritic Stainless Steel (Inox 304, 316, 316L, 310S, 904L, Duplex 2205).
- **Batch Quantity (MOQ):** From 1 master coil/bundle (1 - 3 tons) up to tens of tons, supporting tailored slitting and cut-to-length services.
- **Metallurgical Traceability:** Single Heat Number commitment per batch to ensure consistent springback behavior during CNC bending and pressure vessel fabrication.
- **Quality Assurance:** Full compliance documentation including Mill Test Certificate (Mill Cert) and international CO/CQ standards.

## Industrial Applications
- Industrial tank and pressure vessel fabrication.
- HVAC ventilation systems and large-scale architectural cladding.
- Specialized chemical processing and high-temperature structural components.
